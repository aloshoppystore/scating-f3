Mechanical Innovation

1 Skate = 4 Shoe Sizes

Adjusting the skates takes less than 10 seconds. Here is how simple the mechanism is to operate:

1

Open the lever

Locate the locking lever situated at the bottom-side of the boot heel and flip it upward to release the sizing pin.

2

Pull on the front of the boot

Hold the heel firm and pull on the front toe box of the boot. Expand or push back to slide to the correct size index.

3

Close the lever

Press the lever firmly back down flat against the chassis frame. The size locks in place instantly and safely.

Safety & Maintenance FAQ

Get professional answers about maintenance cycles, rubber braking safety, and gear requirements.

Rubber provides true traction, stopping your child from slipping when pushing off and ensuring effective braking. Plastic just skids on the pavement, takes longer to stop, and wears down rapidly.
We strongly advise that your child always wear a helmet, elbow pads, knee pads, and wrist guards. These protectors buffer falls, protect joints, and give young learners more confidence.
Rotate wheel positions regularly (swap the front wheels to back and swap sides in a cross pattern). Replace them completely when they wear down to the first of the seven visible rings inside the wheel.
